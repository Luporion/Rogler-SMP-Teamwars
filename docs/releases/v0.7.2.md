# v0.7.2 — Team Intel companion HUD (source candidate)

**Scope:** replace previous lack of any client-side team position data with a least-privilege, server-filtered teammate-only stream, client memory lifecycle, and small HUD. Keep existing Xaero fair-play player radar suppression to avoid opponent disclosure. Not a native Xaero render hook.

## Threat model

- FTB Teams effective party UUID is canonical; check viewer and each candidate member independently.
- Never use team name, vanilla scoreboard entry or client-supplied member list for team intel authorization.
- Per-player `PacketDistributor.sendToPlayer`, never broadcast; no location access for solo/unavailable/invalid party.
- Update every 40 ticks, at most 32 members, bounded names and dimension keys; no server-side persistent intel cache.
- Client session is memory-only, sequence-monotonic, expires after 200 client ticks without refresh and is discarded on login/logout. FTB changes are reflected on next update, not instantaneously.
- Existing radar globally disabled; other third-party map/UI mods may leak information independently and require separate audit.
